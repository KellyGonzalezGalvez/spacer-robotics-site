Now Deploying GRID

Autonomous rebar tying, built for real jobsites

GRID is a mobile rebar-tying robot designed to operate directly on rebar grid in job site conditions. It automates repetitive tying while producing reliable output — reducing labor strain, improving schedule reliability, and bringing the cost of the project down.

Process

How it works

01 — Unload
Unload
GRID fits in any SUV trunk. Unloading is easy and takes minutes on any site.
02 — Setup
Prep Setup
GRID is placed on the rebar grid and is ready to work immediately - no complex configuration.
03 — Operate
Detect & Tie
Computer vision identifies intersections and obstacles. GRID autonomously navigates and ties.
04 — Complete
Log & Complete
Work complete. Full QA data logged. Job site is ready for concrete pouring.
